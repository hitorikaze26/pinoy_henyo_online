Getting Started

How to Play

Up and running in three easy steps. No accounts, no downloads—just open, connect, and play.

01

Create Your Teams

Set up your teams before the game begins. Choose how many teams will play, give each team a name, and fill in the words for each category.

02

Connect to the Host

Once your teams are ready, connect each team's phone to the host using the QR code displayed on the laptop.

03

Start Guessing

Once everyone is connected, the host starts the game. Grab your phone, give your clues, and guess as many words as you can before time runs out!
